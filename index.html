About

Turning public data into tools people use

I'm a Data Science student at the University of Calgary with a concentration in mathematics, graduating in April 2027. I like taking data that is hard to use in the form it's published and building something a person can act on, whether that's a dashboard, a pipeline or a ticketing system.

At The City of Calgary I work in information management for Planning & Development Services, where I built the team's first public-facing SharePoint site and its first ticketing system.

Before that I spent a year and a half at the University of Calgary Office of the Registrar, validating a CRM migration of more than 4 million records with SQL and building recruitment dashboards in Power BI.
