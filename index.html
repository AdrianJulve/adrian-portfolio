About

Turning public data into tools people use

I'm a Data Science student at the University of Calgary with a concentration in mathematics. The part of data work I enjoy most is the step between a raw table and a decision: cleaning it, checking it and shaping it into something a person can act on.

At The City of Calgary, I worked in information management for Planning & Development Services, where I built the division's first end-to-end ticketing pipeline and the team's first public-facing SharePoint site. Before that, I spent a year and a half at the Office of the Registrar, helping move millions of student records to a new system.

Outside of work and class, I build my own projects on public data, and I helped lead the Data Science and Machine Learning Club, where I helped launch Vision Labs to connect students with real projects from real companies.
